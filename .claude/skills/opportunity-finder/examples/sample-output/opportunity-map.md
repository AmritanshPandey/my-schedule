## Candidate notes

### AI prior-authorization assistant
Automates prior-auth requests from data already in the schedule
- **Customer:** Top ODI outcome 15.4 strongly underserved [S2]; 9 of 12 interviews [S1]
- **Offer:** Fits as add-on; pricing untested
- **Capabilities & Assets:** Schedule data already captured; unused advantage (VRIO)
- **Market:** Open position affordable + automated; new payer API rules [S5]
- **Strategic Choice:** Product development quadrant, Horizon 2
- **Next:** Run Decision Tree; price test with 15 clinics

### Benchmarking reports from scheduling data
Sell no-show and utilization benchmarks to clinic groups
- **Customer:** Team belief; no customer evidence yet
- **Offer:** Entry rung idea
- **Capabilities & Assets:** Data + analytics recombination; data rights unclear
- **Strategic Choice:** Market development
- **Next:** Explore: 8 interviews using the guide; check data rights

### Do more of what we do today (baseline)
Grow scheduling seats in current segment
- **Customer:** Satisfaction high, few unmet outcomes [S2]
- **Offer:** Proven model
- **Capabilities & Assets:** Existing strengths
- **Market:** Price pressure from bundled suites [S3]
- **Strategic Choice:** Horizon 1 only

### Expand to dental groups
Same product, new vertical
- **Customer:** Similar jobs assumed
- **Market:** Three entrenched dental-specific suites with integrations [S6]
