# Ansoff Growth Matrix

**Lens:** Strategic Choice · **Method 18 of 21** · **Output:** `opportunity-output/18-ansoff-growth-matrix.md`

**Question:** Which growth path fits: more of today, new products, new markets, or both new?

## Why it matters

Each quadrant carries different risk. Naming it makes the bet explicit.

## Use when

- Growth planning; inside-out routes.

## Needs

- Current products and markets
- Candidate opportunities
- Business profile (`opportunity-output/business-profile.md`)

## Steps

1. Populate all four quadrants with at least one concrete option: market penetration, product development, market development, diversification.
2. Rate each option's risk and the evidence still needed.
3. Pick the quadrant to pursue first given resources and ambition, and say why the others wait.

## Checks

- [ ] All four quadrants filled.
- [ ] Risk tied to evidence gaps.
- [ ] Choice explained.
- [ ] Material claims labeled per `references/evidence-rules.md`

## Output

- Ansoff grid with options
- Risk and evidence needed
- Quadrant to pursue first
- **Signals for the map:** 1–3 candidate opportunities this supports or weakens, each with strength (strong / moderate / weak / assumed) and source IDs

## Reading the signal

Options close to existing strengths with evidence score as moderate signals; diversification needs strong evidence elsewhere.
