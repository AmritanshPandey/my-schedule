# Opportunity Solution Tree

**Lens:** Strategic Choice · **Method 20 of 21** · **Output:** `opportunity-output/20-opportunity-solution-tree.md`

**Question:** Which customer opportunities connect to the outcome we want, which solutions could serve them, and what do we test first?

## Why it matters

Teams fall in love with one solution. The tree forces alternatives and points the first experiment at the biggest uncertainty.

## Use when

- A target outcome exists and the team has a favored idea.

## Needs

- Desired outcome (metric)
- Customer opportunities from earlier methods
- Preferred solution, if any
- Business profile (`opportunity-output/business-profile.md`)

## Steps

1. State the outcome precisely and measurably.
2. List at least 3 customer opportunities (needs, pains, desires) that could move it. No solutions at this level.
3. For each, generate 2–3 distinct solutions. Place any preferred solution honestly beside real alternatives.
4. Pick the solution with the highest uncertainty and highest potential impact, and design the smallest experiment to test it (thresholds set in advance).

## Checks

- [ ] Opportunities are needs, not disguised solutions.
- [ ] Multiple solutions per opportunity.
- [ ] Experiment targets the biggest uncertainty.
- [ ] Material claims labeled per `references/evidence-rules.md`

## Output

- Tree (outcome → opportunities → solutions)
- Experiment to run first with thresholds
- **Signals for the map:** 1–3 candidate opportunities this supports or weakens, each with strength (strong / moderate / weak / assumed) and source IDs

## Reading the signal

Solutions with a cheap, decisive experiment move a candidate toward Validate.
