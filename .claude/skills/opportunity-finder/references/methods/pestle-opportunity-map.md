# PESTLE Opportunity Map

**Lens:** Market · **Method 14 of 21** · **Output:** `opportunity-output/14-pestle-opportunity-map.md`

**Question:** Which political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental shifts open or close opportunities for this business?

## Why it matters

Generic PESTLE lists are noise. Only shifts with a direct link to this business matter.

## Use when

- Regulated, policy-exposed or macro-sensitive markets.

## Needs

- Business profile
- Dated external sources
- Business profile (`opportunity-output/business-profile.md`)

## Steps

1. For each category, list only shifts with a direct, stated link to this business. Skip empty categories rather than padding.
2. For each shift: the concrete implication (opening or threat), timing, and source.
3. Pick the shift with the clearest, most timely implication and the move it suggests.

## Checks

- [ ] Only directly linked shifts.
- [ ] Each shift dated and sourced.
- [ ] Implications concrete.
- [ ] Material claims labeled per `references/evidence-rules.md`

## Output

- Relevant shifts table
- Implications
- Shift to act on
- **Signals for the map:** 1–3 candidate opportunities this supports or weakens, each with strength (strong / moderate / weak / assumed) and source IDs

## Reading the signal

A sourced shift creating an opening the business can use is a moderate-to-strong timing signal.

*Method origin: Environmental scanning practice.*
