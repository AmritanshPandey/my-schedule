# Why Now / Technology Shift

**Lens:** Market · **Method 13 of 21** · **Output:** `opportunity-output/13-why-now-shift.md`

**Question:** What has changed that makes this possible or valuable now, and how long will the window stay open?

## Why it matters

Timing is one of the five conditions for an opportunity, and the one teams most often skip. AI capability, cost curves, regulation and behavior all shift what is possible.

## Use when

- Any route, and always for AI-enabled ideas.
- Someone says "we tried this five years ago".

## Needs

- Candidate opportunity
- Dated evidence of changes (technology, cost, regulation, behavior, platforms)
- Business profile (`opportunity-output/business-profile.md`)

## Steps

1. List concrete changes in the last 24 months: new capability (for example, models that can read documents reliably), cost drops, new regulation or reimbursement, behavior shifts, new platforms or APIs. Each with a source and date.
2. For each change, state what it makes newly possible, cheaper or necessary for this business.
3. Ask why this was not done before. If the answer is "nobody thought of it", treat timing as weak.
4. Estimate the window: who else can now do this, and how fast? Label as `[INFERENCE]`.
5. For AI-enabled ideas, note what still does not work well enough and what evaluation would prove it.

## Checks

- [ ] Every change has a dated source.
- [ ] Each change links to a specific capability or demand shift.
- [ ] Window estimate labeled.
- [ ] Material claims labeled per `references/evidence-rules.md`

## Output

- Change log with dates and sources
- What each change enables
- Why not before
- Window and competition for it
- AI readiness notes
- **Signals for the map:** 1–3 candidate opportunities this supports or weakens, each with strength (strong / moderate / weak / assumed) and source IDs

## Reading the signal

A recent, sourced change that removes the old blocker, with few others positioned yet, is a strong "why now" signal.
