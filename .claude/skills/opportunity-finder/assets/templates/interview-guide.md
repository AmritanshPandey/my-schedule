# Interview guide (to test a candidate opportunity)

**Candidate:** · **Segment:** · **Hypotheses to test (from the map):**

Interview 6–10 people per segment. Ask about past behavior, not future intentions. Do not pitch.

## Context (5 min)
1. Tell me about your role and what a typical week looks like for [area].

## The job and the trigger (10 min)
2. Tell me about the last time you had to [job]. What kicked it off?
3. Walk me through what you did, step by step.
4. What tools, people or workarounds did you use?

## The struggle (10 min)
5. Which part took the longest or was most frustrating? Why?
6. What did it cost you (time, money, risk, stress)?
7. Have you tried to fix it? What happened?

## Forces (10 min)
8. What would have to be true for you to change how you do this?
9. What would worry you about switching?
10. What do you pay today to get this done (including people's time)?

## Close (5 min)
11. Who else should I talk to?
12. May I follow up if we build something to test?

## After each interview
- Record verbatim quotes with the interview's source ID.
- Mark which hypotheses gained or lost support. Feed results to `user-research`, then update the map.
